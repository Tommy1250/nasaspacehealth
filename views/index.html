      PRIORITY 1 / EMERGENCY RESPONSE

      Crew member in distress?

      Chest pain, collapse, severe breathing difficulty, or unresponsiveness must be treated as an emergency.

      ACT NOW Alert the other crew members and contact Mission Control using the approved emergency communication procedure. Do not wait for dashboard readings.
      1. Check scene safety. Make sure it is safe to approach.
      2. Check responsiveness and breathing. Follow the crew medical checklist and training.
      3. Activate emergency support. Report symptoms, time of onset, and available readings to Mission Control.
      4. Follow real-time medical direction. Use onboard emergency equipment only as trained and instructed.
      5. Keep monitoring. Record changes and do not leave the crew member alone if it is safe to stay.

      This guide is a training interface, not an autonomous medical decision system. Follow mission-specific medical procedures and professional instructions.
